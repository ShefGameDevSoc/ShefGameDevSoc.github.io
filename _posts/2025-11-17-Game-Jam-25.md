## Thanks to everyone who came to Steel Jam 3, we had a blast!
We hope you enjoyed the free food and snacks, the games and the company. The theme for this jam was ```LOCKED IN```  and we loved what people came up with.


### ETA

We loved how polished this game felt, and loved the interpretation of being locked in a train. I encourage you to play it for yourself :)

![Team](https://shefgamedevsoc.github.io/assets/images/SJ25/e-eta.jpg)

[itch.io link](https://miniminnow.itch.io/eta)


This one won first place taking home £20 a person in Amazon vouckers!

![Winners](https://shefgamedevsoc.github.io/assets/images/SJ25/w-eta.png)

### Deadline

We loved the art design on this game, and really liked the interpretation of being "locked into your studies"

![Team](https://shefgamedevsoc.github.io/assets/images/SJ25/e-dead.jpg)

This one won second place taking home £10 a person in Amazon vouckers!

![Winners](https://shefgamedevsoc.github.io/assets/images/SJ25/w-dead.png)
